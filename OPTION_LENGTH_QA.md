# Initial MCQ option-length quality audit

- 90 distinct original items, all 13 Elementary LS1 English competencies.
- Every set: 30 items, nine Easy, fifteen Moderate, six Challenging.
- Answer key per set: 8 A / 7 B / 8 C / 7 D, without a repeating letter cycle.
- Mean correct-option length: 7.22 words; mean distractor length: 6.96 words.
- Correct choice uniquely longest: 16 out of 90 items.
- Maximum option-length spread: four words; no option spread greater than five words.

**Status:** These are construction checks, not empirical validation. A teacher should check meaning, plausibility, and single-best-answer defensibility. Pilot testing is required to estimate true difficulty and discrimination.
